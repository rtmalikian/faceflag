FaceFlag

Guided selfie-sequence facial-movement pattern triage from landmark ratios only. In this demo, no photo is uploaded: the demo cases are synthetic landmark sets with known asymmetry ratios. A real deployment extracts landmarks on-device from the guided sequence (rest, raise eyebrows, close eyes tight, big smile) and sends only metrics JSON to the gateway. Frames never leave the device.

What it never does: it never says a stroke is ruled out, never diagnoses, and never recommends treatment. A face-only check cannot check balance, eyes, arm or leg strength, or speech. Sudden onset, limb weakness, or speech change always mean emergency care, whatever the face measurements show.
