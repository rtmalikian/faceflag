# EVIDENCE.md — FaceFlag

Every claim below follows the weekly-series evidence standard: Claim | Source
(full citation + link) | Tier | What it proves | What it does NOT prove.
Tier 1 = systematic review / meta-analysis / guideline / authoritative
compendium. Tier 2 = RCT / large cohort / validated real-patient measurement
study. Tier 3 = small observational / pilot. Tier 4 = vendor / press /
synthetic results, labelled as such, never dressed as evidence.

## Honest headline for the solution method

The selfie-to-stroke-triage method itself has no validation study. What exists
is: (1) a textbook anatomical rule with documented exceptions — forehead
pattern is a tendency, not a rule, (2)
landmark-asymmetry severity correlations in peripheral palsy, (3) one tiny
central-vs-peripheral photo classifier (10 central cases), and (4) inpatient
phone asymmetry-detection pilots that explicitly do not solve the
stroke-vs-Bell's distinction. FaceFlag's exact pipeline (guided selfie
sequence -> landmarks -> deterministic ratios -> three zones) has no
published sensitivity/specificity, no threshold validation, and no outcome
study. In plain words: the sensitivity and specificity are unknown.
That sentence belongs in the app, not just in EVIDENCE.md.

## What this app has NOT proven

- Its accuracy on real patients or real selfies is unproven. The eval
  numbers in README are Tier 4 synthetic-fixture results: they prove the
  geometry and zone logic are internally correct on known ground truth,
  not that the product works on patients.
- No threshold in this repo is a validated cut-off. Every zone split is
  labelled APP_DEFINED_UNVALIDATED in code, API and docs, except the
  emergency-escalation posture itself, which follows AHA guidance and the
  documented clinician miss rate.
- No diagnosis, treatment, or risk prediction. Output language is
  screening-pattern language for clinician/emergency review only,
  enforced by banned-language and required-language scans in CI.
- No live-camera claim in v0.1.0. On-device face-mesh extraction is a
  documented integration path, not shipped code: v0.1.0 accepts landmark
  JSON and runs synthetic landmark demos (see docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).
- No regulatory status is claimed. Nothing in this research establishes
  FDA clearance, CE marking, or "medical device" validation. Do not imply
  any of them anywhere until separately verified.
- FaceFlag does not check balance, eyes, arm or leg strength, or speech.
  It must never be described as having "BE-FAST checked" anything.
